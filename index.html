Interactive Learning Platform - Making Learning Fun for Every Child

👩‍🏫 For teachers & parents

This platform has free, interactive learning activities for primary pupils in Mauritius, from Grade 1 to Grade 6.

  • Activities work on computers, tablets and phones.
  • Nothing needs to be installed, and children don't need an account.
  • Younger children can tap 🔊 Listen to hear the instructions on the home page.
  • New activities are added regularly. Look for the NEW! ribbon.

Tip: sit with younger children for the first few activities, then let them explore on their own.
