Land Use Grid Map

Tap a square on the map to see its name (for example B2).
How to read a grid map: the columns have letters (A, B, C, D) at the top. The rows have numbers (1, 2, 3, 4) on the side. Say the letter first, then the number: B2.
Some land uses fill one square. Others fill two squares, side by side ↔ or one above the other ↕.
